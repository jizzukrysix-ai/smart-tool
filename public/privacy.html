Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

1. Information stored on your device

SmartTools Pro stores post drafts, notes, hashtag groups and planner entries in local browser storage on your device. This information is not sent to the SmartTools Pro server. Clearing Telegram or browser site data may delete locally stored information. This starter does not provide cloud synchronization or user accounts.

2. Telegram Mini App

When you open the app inside Telegram, Telegram's Web App SDK provides platform context to the app. This version does not transmit Telegram profile information to an application backend and does not use unvalidated Telegram launch data for server-side identity or authorization.

3. Optional advertising

If you deliberately select the optional ad button, the AdsGram SDK and its advertising providers may process technical information required to request, display and measure an advertisement. Advertising is not automatically requested when the app opens. Core tools remain available if you decline an ad or no ad is available. Please also review the advertising provider's current privacy information.

4. Third-party libraries

The QR generator library is loaded from the cdnjs content delivery network. QR content is processed in the browser by that library. Do not encode confidential or sensitive information in a QR code.

5. Data retention and deletion

Local content remains in the browser storage until the user deletes it or clears the relevant site data. You can delete individual notes, drafts, hashtag groups and planner entries inside the app.
